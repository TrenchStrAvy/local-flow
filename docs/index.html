Sorph
Local transcription model

Sorph listens on your Mac and writes what you say, in 99 languages, as you say it.

Hold a key. Speak. Let go. Your words are already in the field you were typing in.

Everything runs on your machine. No account, no cloud, nothing to pay. The sphere is Sorph listening: it bends with your voice and learns how loud you speak.

Get Sorph

git clone https://github.com/TrenchStrAvy/sorph.git && cd sorph && ./install.sh

macOS 13 or later and Python 3.10 or later. The installer sets up the model, a Sorph runtime app, a Launchpad icon and a login service. Grant Microphone, Accessibility and Input Monitoring once, then hold Right‑Option and speak.
